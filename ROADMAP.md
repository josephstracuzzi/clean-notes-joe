# Roadmap

Ideas agreed for later, in no particular order. When one is built, it moves to CHANGELOG.md.

- **Reset sizing to default**: a quick way to put the text size settings (auto-fit min/max, fixed size) back to
  their defaults without resetting everything else.
- **Per-deck settings**: a deck reopens with the size settings it had last time (for example after a rehearsal),
  so each presentation keeps its own sizing.
- **Clearer source indicator**: make it obvious at a glance whether the notes come from **NETWORK** (and which PC),
  **LOCAL** (PowerPoint on this PC) or **FILE**, shown in the cuelist (slide list) area on the left: a
  prominent source label in its own colour above or around the slide list, rather than relying on the small badge
  on the PGM bar and the line under it.
- **Revisit the fullscreen workflow**: since 0.10.1 the output goes out of sight for a moment when FULLSCREEN is turned
  on or off, or DISPLAY is changed (it's hidden while Windows resizes it, or a fresh window opens on the new screen),
  so it disappears and reappears. It should simply pop in and out of fullscreen in place, without the in-between sizes
  showing and without vanishing. One idea: a borderless window sized to the screen instead of Windows' own fullscreen.
- **Remote slide control**: deliberately not built (network sync is one way). Revisit only if wanted.
